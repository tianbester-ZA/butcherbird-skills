# What's changed

Newest first, in plain words. Every commit to `main` reaches everyone, so write here when something changes how a skill works.

## 10 Nov 2026

- New `/bb:review`: check work against the store before a client sees it, a senior (Tian or Roy) signs off, a 5-day review timeline, and client feedback turned into a list that gets fully done in one round.

## 2 Nov 2026

- First release to the team: `/bb:script`, `/bb:edit`, `/bb:books`.

## 2 November 2026: first version

- Public, so anyone on the team can install it without an account. Three skills: `/bb:script`, `/bb:edit` and `/bb:books`.
- Both working skills now start by reading what's there, saying it back, and asking only what's missing, in one short round. Then they work to the end.
- Taste belongs to the job. An ad is judged on selling, not on the jokes or feel of an organic piece, and the critic judges against the job.
- Works on any team Mac: no fixed paths, and `/bb:edit` checks its tools at the start and gives the exact fix.
- All 17 books read passage by passage. Only the readers' notes and one-page cards ship here, never the books.
