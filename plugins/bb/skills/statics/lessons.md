# Lessons

Dated lines, one per correction, from real rounds. Read them at step 2. No client names in this file; a client's own lessons go in that client's folder. The first set comes from one client's Black Friday statics (16 and 17 Nov 2026), rejected three times before a set passed.

- 16 Nov 2026: a neat type system on a gradient (small eyebrow lines with dot separators, cut-outs on a studio fade) followed every written rule and still wasn't the brand. Start from the pictures that already passed (the client's winners, their designer's best work), not from tokens.
- 16 Nov 2026: one template filled thirteen times read as "not designed, not thought out". One idea and one real scene per ad, and vary the layout across the set.
- 16 Nov 2026: a gift box on another product's ingredients "missed the brief". The background has to belong to the product shown.
- 16 Nov 2026: generated abstract scenes (an eclipse, marble steps) were rejected as off brand and "AI". Real brand photography first; generation only to place the real product in a scene.
- 16 Nov 2026: white text on light photos failed. Type colour follows the ground, and the offer is checked for contrast.
- 16 Nov 2026: a two-colour headline cut a letter in half. A playful move never cuts a letter.
- 16 Nov 2026: a headline that needed explaining ("Encore.") was dropped. Plain words over clever ones.
- 16 Nov 2026: cheeky headlines on unrelated pictures read as random. The headline has to make sense with its picture; two ads were replaced for this.
- 16 Nov 2026: sale dates came off every static. The offer line carries the spend and the value only.
- 16 Nov 2026: left-set logos looked badly designed. Logo top centre, type centred under it unless the scene leaves no room.
- 16 Nov 2026: an offer was illegible on a busy light ground. Quiet the ground behind it with a soft wash; the gate now measures contrast.
- 16 Nov 2026: "spend more, receive more" was a terrible headline, and a small round sticker didn't make a free gift unmissable. The free gift is the hero of the picture, not a badge.
- 16 Nov 2026: the free tiers worked better as a carousel, one tier per card: spend this, receive these.
- 16 Nov 2026: every generated pack came back with garbled label text (one letter off on the brand, invented words on the flavour line). Lay the real packshot over and read every word at zoom.
- 16 Nov 2026: a full real-label overlay looked pasted on, because its tone didn't match the scene. Patch only the wrong strip, or match the tone.
- 16 Nov 2026: a ribbon tied across two jars covered the labels. Props go on the lid or beside the product, never across a label.
- 16 Nov 2026: 1:1 can't be a crop of 9:16. It needs its own layout: usually a one-line headline and offer, the scene moved up.
- 16 Nov 2026: a square browser window tiled the bottom rows of a square render. Render in a window taller than the frame (render.mjs does).
- 17 Nov 2026: SVG text named by font family (like "Geist" instead of "Geist-Black") made the editor look for the Regular weight and swap. Name every font by its PostScript name; export-check now fails a file that doesn't.
- 17 Nov 2026: in VectorCraft, changing a line's text dropped its bold run; and moving a product showed the garbled generated pack underneath. Restyle after editing text, and warn whoever edits.
- 17 Nov 2026: the contact sheet repeated its top rows at the bottom, even after a window-size fix, and the blind test caught it on the stock example. Chrome screenshots of tall or wide pages aren't reliable; the sheets are now drawn onto a canvas of exactly the right size.
- 17 Nov 2026: a blind test as a new designer stalled on day one: copying the example job broke its paths, and fitting new words took four rounds. Jobs now start with new-job.mjs, and the gate prints the number to change.
