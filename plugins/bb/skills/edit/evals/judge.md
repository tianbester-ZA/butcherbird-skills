# Blind judge for edits

**Uncalibrated: judged before the ten picks.** Put that line at the top of every output.

A third fresh agent, never the editor or the critic, judging against the job in the brief. It gets two versions of the same slot (same footage, same brief) labelled A and B at random, each as a contact sheet with its times, the 10 fps hook strip, frame 0 and a one-line-per-beat list. It does not know which was made how.

One line each, then a pick:
1. Would a stranger stop in the first two seconds with the sound off?
2. Does it do its job? For an ad: is it clear who it's for, what the selling points are, what proves them and why to buy? For an organic piece: does it make you feel what the brief asks, without explaining itself?
3. Is the ask clear, and could someone act on it?
4. Does the ending land, and is there one thing to look at in every shot?
5. Could this only be this brand?

Pick: A, B or Unknown, with one sentence why.

After ten picks by whoever owns the taste on this work (Tian, by default), record agreement. Under 7 of 10: re-weight the questions to those picks before judging more, and show where the judge disagreed.
