# $100M Money Models (free course)
**Alex Hormozi.** Read in full, 367 passages.

## The core idea
Most advertising loses money because the payback comes too late. The answer is a planned chain of offers (attraction, upsell, downsell, continuity) that earns more gross profit in the first 30 days than the customer cost to win. Late in the book, the argument moves to stacking these offers so every no has a next step, while keeping the model as simple as possible and advertising the free bonus, not the subscription behind it.

## What this changes
- A generic script opens on the product. We open on the lack, or on the free thing they get.
- A generic script closes on "15% off, limited time". We use a deal big enough to change behaviour, or we make the video about something else.
- A generic script makes the biggest claim it can. We make the claim a stranger will believe, then show proof.
- A generic script gives every product the same shape. We let price against the buyer's money decide the length, and whether it takes one video or a run.
- A generic plan treats a tired ad as needing a new offer. We keep the deal and change how it is described.

## The ten best passages
1. `hormozi-money-0020` · part 1 · A smaller promise can beat a bigger one because more people believe it. · Cut the result claim to what a stranger will accept, then back it with a customer or demo.
2. `hormozi-money-0028` · part 2 · One deal described in new ways feels like a new offer. · Keep the deal fixed and make separate cuts as months free, amount off, percent off and was/now.
3. `hormozi-money-0046` · part 2 · People buy while they feel the lack, not once they are satisfied. · Place the offer at the height of the craving or problem, never after a contented ending.
4. `hormozi-money-0084` · part 4 · Strangers cannot judge value, but they can read a price. · Put the price and what is free or reduced on screen early, and cut the value adjectives.
5. `hormozi-money-0142` · part 6 · A multi-unit discount gets more attention when reframed as free items. · Turn a 33% discount into buy two, get one free in the first line, and check the margin first.
6. `hormozi-money-0167` · part 7 · Selling time depends on price, the audience, and their buying power. · Let a cheap product go straight to purchase in a short cut. Give a dearer one more time, judged against what the buyer can afford.
7. `hormozi-money-0180` · part 8 · A belief breaks in five beats: old belief, why wrong, what's right, why right, proof. · Script a myth-buster in that order, teach the concept rather than the tactic, and end on proof on screen.
8. `hormozi-money-0205` · part 9 · Saying what they don't need makes them believe what you say they do need. · Open on what to skip or not buy, then point to the one pick.
9. `hormozi-money-0238` · part 10 · Repeatedly cutting the price of the same thing tells people your prices mean nothing. · In a sale or follow-up cut, change what's included rather than shaving the same item's price again.
10. `hormozi-money-0310` · part 13 · The free bonus sells better than the membership that pays for it. · Open on the free thing they receive, and reveal the subscription later or on the landing page.

## Three questions for the critic
1. Would a stranger believe the main claim as worded, with its proof on screen? (`0020`)
2. If the video leads on a discount, is it big enough to move someone who otherwise wouldn't buy? (`0026`)
3. Does the video's length suit the price against the buyer's money? (`0167`)

## What doesn't carry over
- **Most of the book:** payback maths, payment plans, penalty trials, fee waivers and cancellation terms belong in the business model or sales room, not a phone video.
- **Live-sale tactics:** menu closes, step-by-step downsells and "how about now?" need a back-and-forth. A video gets one offer and one price (`0356`), with at most one fallback line (`0352`).
- **Long formats:** webinars, challenges and 30-minute pitches don't transfer. Only the idea that dearer things need more trust does (`0169`).
- **Full anchor selling:** a gasp and a sincere pitch take real time (`0223`). In a short video, this shrinks to one price beat, and only if the anchor is real.
- **Pressure tricks:** holding ID as collateral and inventing a house policy (`0264`, `0265`) are not worth copying.
- **Giveaways:** charging for entry makes one an illegal lottery (`0106`).
- **The evidence:** the numbers are mostly his own claims, drawn from gyms and services with high margins. Treat 50% discounts and free offers as things to test against product margin, not as settled for ecommerce.
- **Edits:** the book says nothing useful about feel, rhythm or eye.
