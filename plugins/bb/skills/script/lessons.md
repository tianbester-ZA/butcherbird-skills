# Lessons

Dated bullets. Written when results come in, or when a test teaches something. Each names the ad, the number and its date, the passage behind the beat, and what was tried. Read the last five at step 0; the critic checks the draft against them. Lessons here stay free of client names; a client's own lessons go in that client's folder.

Fixed definitions: hook rate = 3-second plays ÷ impressions. Hold rate = ThruPlays ÷ 3-second plays. Hook under about 20%: re-cut the opening (first frame and first line; the body stays). Hold under about 40%: re-cut the middle, keep the hook. Both healthy and no purchases: the offer or the close, not the edit.

## Seed: the first baseline pack (1 Nov 2026, critic's read, no live numbers)

These came from a critic marking a five-ad sale pack written without the books. They're patterns to check for, not results.

- 2026-11-01 · Five ads, one shape: one person to camera, an off-camera helper, a gentle gag, the deal restated, the same close. Fix: hooks across at least four kinds; vary cast and set-up.
- 2026-11-01 · Spoken, low-energy hooks that fail with the sound off. Fix: picture first; on-screen text where the line carries the hook.
- 2026-11-01 · Hooks that only work if you know the star. The goal was new customers. Fix: the stranger test on every hook.
- 2026-11-01 · Deal said in the body and again in the close; the sale's name never said; scarcity said three ways in one ad. Fix: deal once at the close, one true reason now.
- 2026-11-01 · The client's own two ideas dropped while a slot went to an offer outside the sale window. Fix: list the client's ideas in brief.md; each is used or dropped with a reason.
- 2026-11-01 · Kept from that pack: short exact closes, the deal as the punchline.

## First real jobs (1 Nov 2026: a sale pack and an ad edit; blind judge and critic reads, no live numbers)

- 2026-11-01 · Sale pack blind test: the plainer ad (one table, the star refusing one more, the deal as the reply) beat the busier skit (braai, sprint, two mates, a sound-on hook, a close carrying a list and a price). Fix: the simplest set-up that carries the joke; one message in the close.
- 2026-11-01 · Sale pack: deal-first spare hooks meant the deal said twice whichever hook the edit picked. Fix: the spare hooks don't say the deal.
- 2026-11-01 · Ad edit test: the script promised a result no clip showed. Fix: for edits, every claim names the clip moment that proves it before drafting.

## Tian's reactions (2 Nov 2026, he owns the taste here)

- 2026-11-02 · Rejected a skill built from keyword-searched and sampled books. Fix: every book read passage by passage, and every entry names the passage it rests on.
- 2026-11-02 · Packs listed facts he'd settled as unconfirmed and asked small questions. Fix: settled is settled; pick the sensible reading, log it in the sheet, and only raise what changes a decision or a client.
- 2026-11-02 · Reference reels work on a first-frame line and a mute picture with few words. Fix: don't turn every idea into a talking head, or into people falling in a pool because one reference did.
- 2026-11-02 · Taste belongs to the job. What he liked in one video doesn't carry to another: "The ad is meant to sell." An ad is judged on who it's for, the selling points, proof and a reason to buy, not on the jokes or feel of an organic piece.
- 2026-11-02 · An ad's brief said no winners were on file when they were, nobody asked, and it came out a showcase. Fix: read the client's own evidence first, and ask what's missing before writing.
